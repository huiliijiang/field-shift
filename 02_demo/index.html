Farmers choose next year's crops in autumn, months before they know the summer. This planner doesn't forecast or rank. It shows what a farm can grow, how each option held up in past weather, and what is still unknown.

One demo, two sides. Each side has a switch at the top to move to the other. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
